Emotion Labeling Task

Label 5 tweets by emotion

You'll read 5 short tweets, one at a time, and choose the emotion each one is expressing.

  • Pick one emotion per tweet: anger, fear, joy, love, sadness, or surprise.
  • Go with your first instinct — there's no perfect answer, we want your honest read.
  • A guide with a definition and example for each emotion stays visible on the right the whole time.
  • You can revisit and change any of your 5 answers before you finish.
  • Everyone sees a different random set of 5 tweets, so don't worry about comparing notes.
  • It takes about 2 minutes.
Your responses are saved with your ID so we know who labeled what.
